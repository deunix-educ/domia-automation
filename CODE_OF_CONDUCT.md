# Code of conduct

This project is for people who want their home to be a little smarter, and for those who help build that. Be kind, be patient with beginners, and assume good faith.

Expected: respectful discussion, concrete and honest feedback, credit for other people's work, and accepting that the maintainer may decline a change that does not fit the project.

Not acceptable: harassment or insults, personal attacks, publishing other people's private information (including their addresses, camera images and logs), and trolling.

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, use the private report form of the repository (Security tab), choosing a clear title; it will be handled in confidence.

This text is inspired by the Contributor Covenant (<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/>).
